I am currently a Ph.D. candidate at the School of Management, University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), under the supervision of Professor Xinyu Zhang.

My research interests lie in model averaging, ensemble learning, and distributed learning. In particular, I am interested in understanding the statistical properties of simple and widely used distributed algorithms, developing statistical methods for complex distributed prediction and inference problems, and exploring debiasing techniques for estimation and prediction.

## Research Interests

- Model Averaging
- Ensemble Learning
- Distributed Statistical Learning
